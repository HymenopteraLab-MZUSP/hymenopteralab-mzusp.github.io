# {% include icon.html icon="fa-solid fa-box-archive" %}Collection

The Hymenoptera Collection of the Museum of Zoology of the University of São Paulo holds wasps, bees and ants, including type material of many species. The dashboard below gives an overview of the holdings.

{% include section.html %}

{% include section.html %}

## Data access {#data}

The collection's records are published in open databases, where they can be searched, downloaded and cited.

{% include colecao-dados.html %}

**How to cite:** when using records or specimens, cite the collection as "Hymenoptera Collection, Museu de Zoologia da Universidade de São Paulo (MZUSP)", give the catalog numbers and, when downloading data, the citation and DOI provided by the database.

## Visits, loans and specimen shipments {#visits}

Visits, loans and donations are arranged by email with the curator at [gpcamacho@usp.br](mailto:gpcamacho@usp.br). The collection's procedures are described in the [SOPs](https://github.com/HymenopteraLab-MZUSP/pops-hymenoptera-mzusp) (in Portuguese).
