# Tulu-Tuloi II Expedition

{% include expedicao-topo.html %}

In August 2026, a team of 16 researchers climbed to the summit of Serra do Aracá, in northern Amazonas, to collect ants and other insects on the tepuis. The expedition is the final phase of a FAPESP project on the biodiversity of Amazonian mountains, and the material collected will be deposited at the Museum of Zoology of the University of São Paulo.
