Hymenoptera Laboratory

The Hymenoptera Laboratory at the Museum of Zoology of the University of São Paulo (MZUSP) studies the diversity, evolution and distribution of ants, wasps and bees, and cares for the museum’s Hymenoptera Collection. Here you will find our research lines, team, publications, and the protocols, code and data we produce.
