Team

Researchers, students, technical staff and collaborators of the MZUSP Hymenoptera Laboratory.

Principal Investigator

Gabriela Procópio Camacho Principal Investigator and Curator

Assistant Professor at MZUSP and curator of the Hymenoptera Collection. I study ant systematics, phylogenomics and biogeography, with a focus on the Atlantic Forest.

Kelli dos Santos Ramos Collection Manager

PhD Students

Carlos Daniel Assis dos Santos PhD Student

BSc in Biology (UFES) and MSc in Entomology (UFV). His PhD focuses on the phylogenomics of the fungus-growing ant genus Mycetomoellerius.

Samara Santos PhD Student

MSc from MZUSP with a taxonomic revision of Alfaria (2026). Her PhD focuses on a total-evidence phylogeny of Ectatomminae using UCEs, morphology and fossils. Before her MSc she interned cataloguing the Formicidae types (2022) and digitizing the bee collection (2023).

Davi Vilaça Carvalho MSc Student

Diego Mussolini Matielo MSc Student
